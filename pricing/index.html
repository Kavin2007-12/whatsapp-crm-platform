Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about billing, Meta rates, and onboarding.

Are Meta WhatsApp conversation charges included?

Meta provides 1,000 free service (user-initiated customer support) conversations every month per WhatsApp Business Account. Additional marketing or utility messages are billed directly at official Meta regional rates without any markup.

Can I bring my existing WhatsApp business phone number?

Yes! You can migrate your existing 10-digit mobile number or landline to the official WhatsApp Cloud API. Our engineering team assists with zero downtime verification.

How does the Green Tick verification work?

We submit and manage the official Meta Green Tick badge application directly with Meta Trust & Safety for Growth and Scale tier customers.

Can multiple sales agents log in from different laptops?

Yes. All plans include collaborative agent seats with individual login credentials, round-robin routing, and collision detection from a single WhatsApp number.
